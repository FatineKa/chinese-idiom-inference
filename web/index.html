Live demo · Bayesian idiom retrieval

Which idiom does this sentence describe?

Write a sentence in Chinese. A language model (Qwen2.5-0.5B) scores how well each candidate idiom explains it, a frequency prior is added, and Bayes' rule gives a posterior.

p(idiom | text) ∝ p(text | idiom) · p(idiom)

Demo shortcut: a fast search first keeps the 200 most promising of the 31,114 idioms, taken from the idioms of the most similar training sentences. The correct idiom is among these 200 for 63% of test sentences. Then the exact posterior is computed over those 200. The thesis results use the full dictionary and a larger model.
